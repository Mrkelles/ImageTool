Private by design

There is no ImageTools server receiving your pictures. Processing uses your browser's own image, canvas and file APIs. Close the tab and the in-memory copies are gone. The only thing stored on your device is your light / dark theme choice, where your browser allows it. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. All conversion, PDF generation and compression happen locally in your browser.

Why isn't GIF supported?

GIF was deliberately excluded from this release to keep animated-frame handling honest rather than silently flattening animations.

Can I convert a PNG to SVG?

No — turning pixels into vectors is tracing, a different process. SVG is supported as a source: SVG → JPG / PNG / WebP / AVIF.

Why did AVIF fail in my browser?

Some browsers can decode AVIF but not encode it. When that happens ImageTools tells you plainly instead of returning a mislabelled file.

Is lossless JPEG compression real?

Re-encoding a JPEG always loses a little. In lossless mode ImageTools will not pretend otherwise — see the note in the Compressor.

About ImageTools

ImageTools is a small, focused set of image utilities that run entirely in your web browser: a format converter with 16 dedicated routes, an image-to-PDF maker, and an image compressor.

It was built around three principles: your files stay on your device, results should be honest (real sizes, real savings, no fake “lossless” JPEG), and common jobs should take seconds — drop, choose, download.

Privacy Policy

Local processing. Images you open in ImageTools are processed entirely in your browser using Canvas, File and PDF/ZIP libraries running on your device. They are not uploaded to, stored on, or transmitted to any ImageTools server — there is no image-processing backend.

What's stored. Only your light/dark theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage, and only where the browser allows storage; if storage is blocked, the theme simply won't persist between visits. Image files and results live in tab memory only and disappear when you close the tab. Object URLs created for previews are revoked when files are removed.

Downloads. Converted files, PDFs and ZIPs are generated on your device and saved through your browser's normal download mechanism.

Third parties. The page loads library code (JSZip, jsPDF) and fonts from public CDNs; those providers may see a standard web request for those files, but never your images.
